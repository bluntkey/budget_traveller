Budget Traveller

Find the cheapest way from city 1 to city N with at most K stops and C half-price coupons, then watch the trip play out flight by flight.

A header row is optional. If N is left blank it is the largest city number in the file.

Builds a random flight network that always contains at least one valid route from 1 to N. The graph is also written into the text input so you can reuse it.

Result

-minimum total cost
